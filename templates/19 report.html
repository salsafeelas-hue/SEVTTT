{{ feedback }}

🛡️ Permission Safety Guidelines

  • Check whether a permission is actually required for the application's main function.
  • Avoid automatically allowing every permission requested by an application.
  • Be especially careful with access to contacts, SMS, microphone, camera and location.
  • Prefer limited or temporary permissions when your device provides those options.
