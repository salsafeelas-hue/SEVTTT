🛡 USB Safety Tips

  • Never connect an unknown USB device to your computer.
  • Do not open unknown files or programs from removable media.
  • Report found USB devices to an administrator or IT department.
  • Attackers may intentionally leave USB devices in public places.
  • Curiosity and urgency can be used to manipulate victims.
